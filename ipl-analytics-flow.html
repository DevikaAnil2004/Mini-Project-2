From raw data to dashboard

The analytical pipeline behind the project, start to finish.

01

Collect

Ball-by-ball and match-level IPL datasets sourced from Kaggle.

02

Clean

Structuring and preprocessing with Pandas and NumPy.

03

Explore

EDA to surface top scorers, wicket-takers, and venue effects.

04

Visualize

Bar charts, line graphs, heatmaps, and pie charts built in Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Plotly.

05

Present

Insights compiled into one interactive analytics dashboard.
